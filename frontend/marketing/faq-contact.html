IntenseFX Trades is a trading journal, analysis, backtesting and simulated prop-firm practice platform. You record or import trades, review them in a journal, study your performance, replay historical XAUUSD candles and practise in simulated accounts.

It is a practice and analysis tool. It is not a broker and does not place real orders.

Dashboard, Trades, Trading Journal, Performance analytics, Trade Analysis, a rule-based AI Coach, Backtesting with replay, a chart workspace with drawing tools and technical indicators, trading accounts, and Prop Firm Practice. Which ones you can open depends on your plan. See the Features page for a screenshot tour.

Simulated, prop-firm-style practice accounts inside the app (for example a "2 Step Standard · Phase 1 · $5,000" practice account) with a XAUUSD practice chart and account analytics such as balance, equity and loss limits remaining. It helps you rehearse process and discipline.

It does not connect to, mirror or control any external prop firm's challenge. Read more.
