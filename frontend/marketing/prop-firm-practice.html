Practice accounts

Why gamble when you can practise?

The Prop Firm Session workspace lets you open a simulated practice account and see what a challenge asks of you, without a real fee or real capital.

  • Practice accounts by model and phase, e.g. “2 Step Standard · Phase 1 · $5,000”
  • Account size, start and end dates, today's profit
  • Balance and equity, plus daily and total loss remaining
  • A practice score and account analytics

How it works

Five steps from rehearsal to review.

Every step below exists in the app today. Nothing here connects to, or controls, an external prop firm's real challenge. Prop Firm Practice opens with plans that include Backtesting (Starter and above).

  1. Choose the practice environment In the app

    Open Prop Firm Session and create a practice account for the model and phase you want to rehearse.

  2. Review the account settings In the app

    Check account size, dates and the loss limits shown for that practice account before you place a trade.

  3. Practise on the chart In the app

    Open the XAUUSD practice chart, replay candles and place simulated orders with the supported tools.

  4. Review decisions and results In the app

    Closed trades flow into your trade list, where you can add review notes, strategy and tags.

  5. Analyse and improve In the app

    Use the practice analytics, performance and trade analysis views to see where discipline slipped.

Preparation self-check

Mindset

Trade like an analyst. Not like a gambler.

Less impulse

  • Enters because the candle “looks right”
  • Moves the stop after the trade goes wrong
  • Judges the day by the last trade
  • Never looks at the numbers

More intelligence

  • Writes the reason down before entering
  • Reviews each trade against a checklist
  • Studies the pattern across many trades
  • Changes one thing at a time

LESS IMPULSE. MORE INTELLIGENCE. The journal and performance views are built for exactly this.
