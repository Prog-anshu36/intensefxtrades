Honest limits.

What to know before you start:

  • Everything is simulation. Orders are not sent to any broker.
  • Trades, journals and settings are stored in your browser on one device. There is no cloud sync yet.
  • XAUUSD replay uses the real supplied dataset; other symbols use demo data.
  • The AI Coach is rule-based and runs locally. No AI model is connected.
  • Traders Lounge, Trade Rooms and Leaderboard appear in the app as inactive “coming soon” items.
