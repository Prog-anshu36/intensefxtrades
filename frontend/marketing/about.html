About IntenseFX

Who we are.

IntenseFX Trades is a trading analysis and practice platform built to help traders develop discipline, improve their understanding of the markets, and make more informed trading decisions.

We started with a simple purpose: to help traders who are constantly trying to improve but struggle to make consistent progress. We believe better trading begins with structured practice, clear analysis, and a deeper understanding of risk.

Our platform brings together trading charts, historical market data, profit analysis, strategy testing, and simulated prop-firm evaluation practice in one place.

Purpose

Our mission.

Our mission is to help traders prepare more effectively for prop-firm evaluations and work toward their payout goals through disciplined practice and performance analysis.

With IntenseFX Trades, users can practice multiple evaluation days and multiple account challenges within a single day or a few hours using historical market data. This accelerated practice helps traders review their decisions, understand evaluation requirements, and assess their readiness before committing to real challenges.

We aim to give traders the performance insights they need to understand their strengths and weaknesses, manage their trading approach, and make more informed decisions as they work toward their goals.

Looking ahead

Our vision.

We envision a future where traders can prepare confidently, practice systematically, and understand their performance before risking money on real evaluations.

Our goal is to make IntenseFX Trades a trusted destination for trading analysis and practice—helping users replace guesswork with data-driven learning and disciplined preparation.

The platform

What we offer.

01

Trading Charts and Historical Market Data

Explore historical price movements through clear, accessible charts. Review market conditions and study how strategies perform across different periods.

02

Profit and Performance Analysis

Understand trading results through profit and loss metrics, charts, and performance insights. Use your data to identify strengths, weaknesses, and opportunities to improve.

03

Prop-Firm Evaluation Practice

Practice simulated 15-day and 30-day evaluation challenges using historical market data. Test multiple challenges and accounts in a single day, review evaluation objectives, and become more familiar with risk rules.

04

Strategy Testing and Backtesting

Test strategies against historical data, analyze outcomes, and use the results to guide further practice and refinement.

How we work

Our approach.

IntenseFX Trades helps users:

  • Review multiple evaluation days in a shorter period.
