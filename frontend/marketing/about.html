The problem it addresses

Many traders pay for challenges or risk capital before they have a tested process and an honest record of their own behaviour. Practice, journaling and analysis tools aim to close that gap.

Our approach

Practise in a simulation, record every trade with its context, and study the pattern across many trades. The platform is built around discipline and analysis rather than predictions.
