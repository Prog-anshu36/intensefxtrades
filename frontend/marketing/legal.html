Privacy Policy

The full policy is shown in the app: open the Privacy Policy in the app. In summary:

  • When hosted authentication is configured, Supabase Auth stores your email address and sign-in details. Passwords are handled by the authentication provider and are not stored by the app.
  • Trades, journals, drawings and settings are saved in your browser on your device. They are not uploaded to the account database and do not automatically follow you to another browser or device. Clearing browser storage can remove them.
  • This marketing website has no accounts and does not collect passwords or payment details. The contact form, when connected, only sends the name, email and message you type.
