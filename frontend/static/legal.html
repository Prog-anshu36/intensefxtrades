Terms of Service

Use of the app

intensefxTRADES provides trade journaling, charting, and historical backtesting tools for informational and educational use. It is not a broker, exchange, investment adviser, or order-routing service. Chart orders are simulated unless a separately documented integration says otherwise.

Historical data can be delayed, incomplete, adjusted, or unavailable. Backtest results are simulations and do not predict future results. You are responsible for checking data and decisions before relying on them.

Keep your sign-in credentials private and use the app lawfully. Do not attempt to disrupt the service, access another person's account, or bypass access controls. Features and availability may change as the product is developed.

Privacy Notice

Information and storage

When hosted authentication is configured, Supabase Auth processes your email address and sign-in credentials. intensefxTRADES stores your profile name and account plan in its Supabase database. Passwords are handled by the authentication provider and are not stored by the app.

In the current GitHub Pages edition, trades, journals, drawings, and settings are saved in this browser on this device. They are not uploaded to your Supabase profile and do not automatically follow you to another browser or device. Clearing browser storage can remove them.

Google sign-in, when enabled by the app owner, shares account information according to the Google and Supabase privacy policies. This app does not currently process subscription payments.

For account-data requests, use the account controls or contact the site owner through the channel that provided this app.
